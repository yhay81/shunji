# Dictionary source and license

Dictionary entries are derived from **JMdict English** by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG).

- Source: https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project
- Download: https://www.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMdict_e.gz
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The build converts JMdict XML into compact JSON entry records, Japanese spelling/reading indexes, and an English FTS5 index. It does not add proprietary dictionary definitions.
