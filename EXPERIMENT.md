# 30-day continuation gate

Evaluate only real use; automated QA is reported separately.

Continue when all are met during a rolling 30-day window:

- at least 30 real visitors;
- at least 15 visitors complete a successful search;
- at least 8 visitors save a word card;
- at least 5 visitors return on another JST day;
- successful searches are at least 70% of searches.

Before changing the interface, inspect no-result rate and only aggregate action counts. Search text must never be collected to improve the metric.
